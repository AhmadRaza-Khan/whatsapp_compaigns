# WhatsApp Campaigns: quick guide

Log in at **https://DOMAIN**. Forgot your password? Click "Forgot your password?" on the login page.

## 1. Add your customers

**Contacts > Import** and upload an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file with a **phone** column (and optionally **name**, **email**, **tags**).
- Local numbers like `5512 3456` automatically become `+974 5512 3456`.
- Choose **New group…** to put everyone from the file into a group (e.g. "VIP customers").
- After import you see how many were added, and a list of duplicates and invalid numbers you can download and fix.

You can also add, edit, tag or delete contacts one by one, and **Export** the list at any time.

## 2. Check your templates

WhatsApp only allows approved message templates for promotions. **Templates** shows them with a preview. To make a new one, click **New template**, fill in the message, and submit. WhatsApp usually approves within a day. Click **Sync from Bird** to refresh the status.

## 3. Create a campaign

**Campaigns > New campaign**
1. Name it (only you see the name) and pick an approved template.
2. Fill in the blanks: the **image link**, the **collection name**, the **offer**, and the **Shop Now link** (your shop page). Click **+ Name** to greet each customer by first name.
3. Choose the **groups** (or tags) to send to. Customers who replied STOP are left out automatically.
4. Check the **recipient count** and **estimated cost** on the right.
5. Enter your own number and click **Send test** to see exactly what customers will get.
6. Click **Send or schedule**: send now, or pick a date and time (Qatar time).

While it sends you can **Pause**, **Resume** or **Cancel**.

## 4. See the results

Open the campaign to see **sent, delivered, read, clicked (Shop Now), replies, failed** (with the reason) and the **actual cost** from Bird. Use the filters to see, for example, who clicked, and **Export CSV** for Excel. The **Dashboard** shows totals.

Customers' replies are listed under **Replies**. Answer them from the WhatsApp Business app on your phone as usual.

## Good to know

- **STOP**: a customer who replies STOP (or taps "Stop promotions") is never messaged again.
- **Sending speed** depends on your Bird plan (e.g. 30 per minute). 2,500 customers take about 1.5 hours on Startup.
- **Costs** come out of your Bird balance. Keep it topped up; if it runs out, the campaign pauses and resumes when you click **Resume**.
- Staff users can do contacts and campaigns; only admins can change settings and users.
